RAD JARVIS Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

This policy explains how RAD JARVIS, the private desktop assistant used by Rad Electric Co ("we"), handles information when its owner connects his Google Account. RAD JARVIS runs only on the owner's own Windows computer and is used only by Rad Electric Co.

Information RAD JARVIS accesses

With the owner's permission through Google's sign-in, RAD JARVIS accesses:

  • The list of the owner's Google calendars: names, which one is primary, and whether each can be edited.
  • Events on the calendars the owner selects: titles, dates and times, locations, descriptions, guests and their responses.
  • The owner's Google account email address, used only to confirm the right account is connected.

It does not access Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, calendar settings or sharing permissions.

How the information is used

  • To answer the owner's questions, such as "What's going on tomorrow?" or "Am I free Friday at one?", and to give short daily and weekly briefings.
  • To point out clashes between the owner's calendar and his work schedule. The two are compared on the computer and are never copied or synchronised.
  • To prepare calendar changes the owner asks for. A change is made only after the owner reviews the exact details on screen and presses Approve. Each change is then checked by reading the calendar back.
  • To give the owner reminders and alerts about upcoming events.

The information is not used for advertising, profiling, selling, or training artificial-intelligence models.

Where the information is processed and stored

  • RAD JARVIS processes calendar information on the owner's computer, including with its local AI model, which also runs on that computer. Rad Electric Co operates no server that receives this information.
  • The Google sign-in tokens are stored on that computer, encrypted with Windows data protection tied to the owner's Windows account.
  • Calendar events are read when needed and held only briefly in memory (about one minute for events, ten minutes for the calendar list).
  • A limited amount is kept on the computer, encrypted, so RAD JARVIS can work reliably:
    • a record of calendar changes the owner prepared or approved (event title, time and outcome);
    • recent alert cards, kept up to seven days after they are dismissed;
    • reminders the owner links to an event.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share Google user data, and we use no analytics or advertising services with it. Apart from RAD JARVIS's own requests to Google Calendar, calendar information stays on the owner's computer.

External AI services. RAD JARVIS can optionally connect to an external AI service (OpenAI's ChatGPT). That connection is not authorized, so no information is sent to it; RAD JARVIS uses only its local AI model on the owner's computer. If the external connection were ever authorized, recent conversation text could be sent to it, and that text may include calendar details RAD JARVIS has just spoken. For that reason, the connection will stay unauthorized until RAD JARVIS is updated to exclude Google Calendar data from anything sent to an external AI service. This policy will be updated before any such change.

Google API Services User Data Policy

RAD JARVIS's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

  • Disconnecting Google Calendar in RAD JARVIS revokes the access with Google and deletes the stored sign-in from the computer.
  • Access can also be removed at any time in the Google Account under Security → Third-party apps with account access.
  • Reminders can be deleted in RAD JARVIS's settings. All locally kept records, including the approval history and alert cards, are deleted by removing RAD JARVIS's data from the computer.

Security

Google sign-in uses Google's official browser page, with PKCE. Tokens are encrypted on the computer. Calendar changes require on-screen approval tied to the exact details shown, expire after ten minutes and can be used only once.
