RAD JARVIS

RAD JARVIS is the private desktop assistant used by Rad Electric Co, a licensed electrical contractor in Riverside County, California. It runs on the company owner's own Windows computer. It is not offered to the public, it is not sold, and there is nothing to download from this website.

What it does

  • Answers spoken and typed questions about the owner's day: appointments, reminders and work schedule.
  • With the owner's permission, reads the owner's own Google Calendar to say what is coming up and whether a time is free.
  • Can prepare a new, moved or cancelled calendar event. Nothing changes until the owner reviews the exact details on screen and presses Approve. A spoken "yes" never approves a change.

Google Calendar access

RAD JARVIS asks Google for two permissions: to see the list of the owner's calendars, and to view and edit events. It does not ask for access to Gmail, contacts, calendar settings or sharing. The owner can disconnect at any time in RAD JARVIS, which revokes and deletes the access, or remove it from the Google Account's security settings.

RAD JARVIS keeps Google Calendar separate from Rad Electric Co's job-management system. It may compare the two to point out a scheduling clash, but it never copies or synchronises information between them.
